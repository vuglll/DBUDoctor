# DBU013: Large Databricks App

Severity: info. Introduced in 0.7.

An app sets a `compute_size` larger than MEDIUM.

## Why it matters

"Apps are billed per hour of compute time while running, based on provisioned capacity" ([Databricks Apps](https://docs.databricks.com/aws/en/dev-tools/databricks-apps/), checked 2026-11-09). The size sets the rate, per instance: MEDIUM 0.5, LARGE 1 and XLARGE 3 DBU per hour ([compute size](https://docs.databricks.com/aws/en/dev-tools/databricks-apps/compute-size)). LARGE costs 2× MEDIUM and XLARGE 6×, all month, whether or not anyone uses the app. The message gives the monthly cost from DBUDoctor's app pricing.

## Example

```yaml
resources:
  apps:
    portal:
      compute_size: XLARGE   # DBU013: 6× MEDIUM
```

## How to fix

Use MEDIUM unless the app needs more CPU or memory under load, and measure first. Stop apps nobody uses.

## When to suppress

Suppress when the app needs the larger size under its real load:

```yaml
suppressions:
  - rule: DBU013
    resource: apps.portal
    reason: Load test needs 12 vCPUs at peak (PERF-21)
```
