# Code of conduct

DBUDoctor follows the [Contributor Covenant, version 2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/).

In short: be respectful and constructive, assume good intent, and focus on the work. Harassment, personal attacks and discriminatory language are not accepted in issues, pull requests, discussions or any other project space.

## Reporting

Report unacceptable behavior privately to the maintainer, [@vuglll](https://github.com/vuglll), through GitHub. Reports are kept confidential. The maintainer may edit or remove comments, and may block people who break this code, as described in the Contributor Covenant's enforcement guidelines.
